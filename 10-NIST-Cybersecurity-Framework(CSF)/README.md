# NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

## Project Overview

This project provides an overview of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) and its six core functions.

The framework helps organizations manage cybersecurity risks, strengthen security practices, and improve their ability to prevent, detect, respond to, and recover from cyber incidents.

This project was created as part of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate.

---

## What Is the NIST Cybersecurity Framework?

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) is a set of guidelines and best practices developed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

It helps organizations identify cybersecurity risks, protect critical assets, detect security events, respond to incidents, and recover from cyberattacks.

The framework is widely used by organizations of all sizes to improve their cybersecurity posture.

---

# The Six Functions of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework

## 1. Govern

The Govern function establishes the organization's cybersecurity strategy, policies, responsibilities, and risk management processes.

### Why It Is Important

- Defines security objectives.
- Supports decision-making.
- Ensures accountability.
- Aligns cybersecurity with business goals.

---

## 2. Identify

The Identify function helps organizations understand their assets, systems, data, and cybersecurity risks.

### Activities Include

- Identifying critical assets.
- Assessing cybersecurity risks.
- Understanding business requirements.
- Managing assets.

### Why It Is Important

Organizations cannot protect what they do not know they have.

---

## 3. Protect

The Protect function focuses on implementing safeguards that reduce the likelihood of cybersecurity incidents.

### Examples

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Access control
- Security awareness training
- Data encryption
- Regular software updates

### Why It Is Important

Protective measures reduce the risk of successful cyberattacks.

---

## 4. Detect

The Detect function enables organizations to quickly identify cybersecurity events and suspicious activity.

### Examples

- Security monitoring
- SIEM tools
- Log analysis
- Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)

### Why It Is Important

Early detection allows security teams to respond before an incident causes significant damage.

---

## 5. Respond

The Respond function focuses on managing cybersecurity incidents after they have been detected.

### Typical Activities

- Incident analysis
- Containment
- Communication
- Investigation
- Mitigation

### Why It Is Important

An effective response helps reduce the impact of cyber incidents and restore normal operations more quickly.

---

## 6. Recover

The Recover function focuses on restoring systems, services, and business operations after a cybersecurity incident.

### Examples

- Restoring data from backups.
- Disaster recovery.
- Business continuity planning.
- Improving security after an incident.

### Why It Is Important

Recovery ensures that organizations can return to normal operations while learning from previous incidents.

---

## Benefits of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework

Organizations use the NIST Cybersecurity Framework to:

- Improve cybersecurity risk management.
- Protect critical systems and information.
- Strengthen security controls.
- Improve incident response.
- Support business continuity.
- Continuously improve cybersecurity practices.

Its six core functions help organizations build strong security programs and improve resilience against cyber threats.
